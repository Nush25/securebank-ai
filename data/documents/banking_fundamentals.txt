
BANKING FUNDAMENTALS

1. TYPES OF BANK ACCOUNTS

Savings Account:
A savings account is used by individuals to deposit money, withdraw funds and earn interest according to the bank's terms.

Current Account:
A current account is generally designed for businesses and frequent transactions. Its features and charges depend on the bank.

Fixed Deposit (FD):
A fixed deposit allows customers to deposit a lump sum for a selected period at an agreed interest rate.

Recurring Deposit (RD):
A recurring deposit allows customers to deposit a fixed amount regularly for a selected period.

2. LOANS AND CREDIT

Personal Loan:
A personal loan is generally an unsecured loan used for personal expenses. Approval depends on the lender's eligibility criteria.

Home Loan:
A home loan is used to purchase, construct or renovate a residential property, subject to the lender's conditions.

Education Loan:
An education loan helps finance eligible educational expenses. Eligibility, coverage and repayment terms depend on the lender.

EMI:
EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is a fixed periodic payment towards repaying a loan, consisting of principal and interest.

Credit Score:
A credit score is a numerical indicator of a borrower's credit history. Lenders may use it when evaluating loan applications.

3. DIGITAL BANKING

Internet Banking:
Internet banking allows customers to access banking services through a bank's website.

Mobile Banking:
Mobile banking allows customers to access banking services through a bank's mobile application.

UPI:
Unified Payments Interface (UPI) enables users to make instant bank-to-bank payments through supported applications.

NEFT:
National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT) is an electronic system for transferring money between bank accounts.

RTGS:
Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) is an electronic funds transfer system that processes transactions individually in real time.

IMPS:
Immediate Payment Service (IMPS) enables instant electronic fund transfers.

4. BANK CARDS

Debit Card:
A debit card allows customers to make payments or withdraw cash using funds available in their linked bank account.

Credit Card:
A credit card allows eligible customers to borrow funds up to an assigned credit limit and repay according to the card's terms.

ATM:
An Automated Teller Machine (ATM) allows customers to perform supported banking transactions, including cash withdrawals.

5. KYC AND CUSTOMER VERIFICATION

KYC stands for Know Your Customer.

Banks use KYC procedures to verify customer identity and address.

Depending on applicable requirements, banks may request identity documents, address proof, photographs and other information.

Customers should provide documents only through official and verified banking channels.

6. BANKING SECURITY

Customers should:
- Keep passwords, PINs and OTPs confidential.
- Never share sensitive banking credentials with unknown people.
- Avoid clicking suspicious banking links.
- Use official banking websites and applications.
- Report suspicious or unauthorized transactions promptly.
- Verify payment details before confirming transactions.

7. BANKING TRANSACTIONS

NEFT, RTGS and IMPS are different electronic money transfer systems.

UPI supports instant payments through participating banks and applications.

Transaction status, processing times, applicable charges and limits depend on the payment system, bank and applicable rules.

8. CUSTOMER COMPLAINTS

Customers should first report banking issues through their bank's official customer support or grievance redressal channels.

Customers should retain transaction references, complaint numbers and relevant communication.

If an issue remains unresolved, customers may use the applicable regulatory grievance redressal mechanism.

9. IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Bank-specific interest rates, charges, transaction limits, loan amounts, eligibility requirements and processing times must be verified using the relevant bank's current official documents.

This document provides general banking education and does not establish the policies of any particular bank.

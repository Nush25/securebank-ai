# Banking Policy Document

## 1. Savings Account

A savings account allows customers to deposit money and earn interest.

- Customers can deposit and withdraw money according to their account terms.
- Banks must clearly communicate applicable fees and charges.
- Interest rates may change according to the bank's published policy.

## 2. Account Security

- Customers must keep their passwords, PINs and OTPs confidential.
- Banks must use appropriate security measures to protect customer information.
- Customers should report suspicious transactions immediately.
- Banks must never ask customers to share their OTP or PIN through unsolicited calls or messages.

## 3. Loan Eligibility

- Loan approval depends on income, credit history and repayment capacity.
- Banks may request identity and income documents.
- Interest rates and repayment schedules must be communicated before loan acceptance.

## 4. Transaction Disputes

- Customers should report unauthorized transactions as soon as possible.
- Banks must investigate reported disputes according to applicable regulations and internal procedures.
- Customers should retain transaction references and complaint numbers.

## 5. Customer Privacy

- Customer information must be protected from unauthorized access.
- Personal information should only be used for legitimate banking purposes.
- Access to customer records must be restricted to authorized personnel.
